# Channel style notes (inspiration only; never copy a channel's scripts, titles or footage)

## RicksClips (from the engine skill's story formula)
Story-first explainers. Beats: 1) state an odd act as a plain fact in line 1 and show its consequence in the first 3 s; 2) give context: why they were there; 3) a doubter mocks it with one funny quoted line; 4) the hero replies, short and stubborn; 5) early evidence favours the doubters, time ticks; 6) the disaster or turn hits the doubter; 7) the science in one line with a real number, plus one everyday analogy; 8) end on a line that loops back into line 1.
Prefer 2-4 people around one object or place; no fast physical action; no animals as leads.
