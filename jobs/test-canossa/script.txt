In January 1077, the most powerful king in Europe stood outside a mountain castle in the snow.
Henry the Fourth had been cut off from the Church by Pope Gregory the Seventh, and his nobles were turning against him.
He came to Canossa to beg forgiveness, and for three days he waited outside the gates in simple clothes.
Inside, the pope finally agreed to see him and lifted the ban.
Henry kept his crown, but the story showed that a pope could humble a king.
