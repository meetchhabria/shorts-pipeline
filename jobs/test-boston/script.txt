In December 1773, a crowd of colonists crept aboard three ships in Boston Harbor.
They were angry about a British tax on tea, and about having no say in how they were taxed.
That night, men disguised as Mohawk warriors smashed open hundreds of tea chests with hatchets.
Three hundred and forty-two chests of tea were thrown into the cold water.
Nobody was hurt, but Britain answered with harsh new laws that pushed the colonies toward revolution.
