In 1859, two empires almost went to war over a pig, as on San Juan Island an American farmer kept finding a British pig digging up his potatoes.
One day, he'd had enough—bang—the pig's owner wanted him arrested, so America sent soldiers, Britain sent warships, and soon over two thousand men were camped on one tiny island glaring at each other.
For twelve years, not one person was hurt, until the Kaiser of Germany settled it and the island went to America.
Total casualties: one pig.
